04 / THE BREEZY BLUEPRINT

MORE THAN A COMPETITION.
A BIGGER SPORTING VISION.

The EKSUMSA League has always been about more than the final score. It is about participation, identity, competition, recognition and the experiences that bring students together.

We believe there is room to take things further: a more engaging sporting environment, a more inclusive culture, new possibilities for student participation and more reasons to be involved.

There is a bigger picture being developed for EKSUMSA sports. Some ideas are taking shape. Others are being carefully worked on.
