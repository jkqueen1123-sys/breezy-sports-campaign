MANIFESTO & STRATEGIC ACTION PLAN

Seven pillars for a more active, inclusive, competitive and connected sporting community. A practical programme for building sporting opportunity, organisation, performance and community across EKSUMSA.

AYAMOLOWO BOLUWATIFE RUBEN · BREEZY2026 / 2027 SESSION

THE DIRECTION

A CLEAR VISION.
A PRACTICAL PLAN.

This manifesto is designed to move EKSUMSA sports forward through systems that can be organised, measured, reviewed and handed over.

THE VISION

To build an accessible, organised, competitive and sustainable sporting culture in EKSUMSA - one in which students across academic levels and sporting interests have meaningful opportunities to participate, develop, represent the association and celebrate sporting excellence.

THE MISSION

To strengthen EKSUMSA sports through better equipment and resource management, structured grassroots training, well-coordinated competitions, institutional and technical partnerships, digital innovation, effective media coverage and a vibrant sporting community.

GUIDING PRINCIPLES

ACCESSIBILITY & INCLUSION

Opportunities for interested students across academic levels, including female students and students based at different training locations.

FAIRNESS & TRANSPARENCY

Clear participation, selection, officiating, award and resource-allocation processes.

ACCOUNTABILITY

Reliable records of equipment, activities, event outcomes, expenditure and handovers.

COLLABORATION

Work with class representatives, clubs, departments, university teams, other MSAs and relevant external partners.

FEASIBILITY

Match commitments to available time, funding, personnel, facilities and approvals.

CONTINUITY

Build systems, records and partnerships that future administrations can sustain.

01

PILLAR 01

Sports Infrastructure & Equipment Development

Building the foundation for a more accessible, equipped and sustainable sporting culture in EKSUMSA.

STRATEGIC OBJECTIVE

Improve access to essential sporting equipment, establish responsible systems for its management and maintenance, and pursue better access to sporting facilities so EKSUMSA activities can be organised more efficiently and consistently.

01.1

Class-Based Football Equipment Provision

Work towards an initial allocation of at least one football to each class, subject to a needs assessment, available resources and the final number of classes covered.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Use class sports representatives as custodians responsible for proper use, storage and reporting. Maintain separate match-day supplies for EKSUMSA League fixtures and a separate training supply for representative teams, so goalkeepers and outfield players can train simultaneously when required.

EXPECTED IMPACT

More consistent class access to football equipment, smoother league match operations and better-supported representative-team training.

01.2

Progressive Acquisition of Sporting Equipment

Acquire additional sporting equipment through a phased, needs-based approach informed by participation, existing inventory and programme priorities.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Prioritise active sports and scheduled programmes. Planned items include footballs, cones, bibs and pumps; basketballs and accessories; volleyballs, nets and boundary markers; badminton shuttlecocks; table-tennis bats, balls and nets; athletics batons, measuring tapes, stopwatches and markers; and indoor-game materials such as chess sets and Scrabble boards. Expand procurement as resources permit.

EXPECTED IMPACT

A wider range of sporting activities can be supported without committing the Directorate to purchasing every item at once.

01.3

Equipment Management, Maintenance & Accountability

Create a reliable system for tracking, storing, maintaining and handing over sporting equipment.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Maintain an inventory showing description, quantity, acquisition date, condition, custodian and maintenance or replacement history. Introduce issue-and-return records, appropriate storage, routine condition checks and an updated equipment register for the Directorate handover.

EXPECTED IMPACT

Reduced loss and misuse, improved equipment lifespan and clearer accountability for association resources.

01.4

Assessment, Advocacy & Access to Sporting Facilities

Assess available sporting facilities and advocate for appropriate access, maintenance and improvement through the relevant authorities.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Identify facilities available to EKSUMSA and document limitations. Engage university authorities and facility managers, explore shared-use arrangements or facility partnerships, and communicate needs with practical evidence and realistic proposals.

EXPECTED IMPACT

A clearer understanding of facility needs and stronger engagement with the bodies responsible for sporting infrastructure. Physical upgrades remain subject to authority approval and resources.

01.5

Sustainable Sports Resource Development

Build a more dependable resource base for sporting activities through responsible fundraising and partnership development.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Prepare sponsorship and partnership proposals for suitable organisations, businesses, alumni and other potential supporters. Explore equipment donations, event sponsorship and institutional support; report transparently on support received and document useful contacts for continuity.

EXPECTED IMPACT

A stronger foundation for equipment, training and events without assuming that sponsorship or external funding is guaranteed.

MEASURABLE DELIVERABLES

  • Class football-allocation and custody register, with separate counts for class balls, league match-day supplies and representative-team training supplies.
  • Consolidated sporting-equipment inventory with condition and maintenance records.
  • Documented equipment-needs and procurement-priority list.
  • Facility-access assessment and record of engagement with relevant authorities or partners.
  • Record of sponsorship, donation and resource-development proposals pursued.
02

PILLAR 02

Grassroots Sports Development & Participation

Creating an inclusive, organised and active sporting community where every EKSUMITE has the opportunity to participate, develop and compete.

STRATEGIC OBJECTIVE

Establish a dependable grassroots structure that encourages regular participation, improves sporting skills and ensures accessible opportunities across academic levels, experience and gender.

02.1

Appointment of Sports Captains and Coaches

Establish a coordination structure across available sporting disciplines.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Appoint sports captains and identify suitable student coaches or training leads according to interest, experience and competence. Define responsibilities for coordinating participation, supporting training, communicating needs and reporting challenges. Encourage preparation for inter-class, association and external competitions; review the structure as participation grows.

EXPECTED IMPACT

Clearer coordination, stronger student ownership and more consistent leadership across sporting disciplines.

02.2

Structured Weekly Sports Training Programme

Coordinate regular training opportunities for interested EKSUMITES across available sports.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Develop weekly schedules with captains and coaches, considering facility access and the academic calendar. Keep sessions open to interested students across levels; distinguish open training from representative-team selection. Use sessions for instruction, drills, practice, fitness and friendly matches. Adjust around lectures, clinical postings and examinations; add representative-team preparation when required.

EXPECTED IMPACT

More regular participation, improved basic skills and a clearer pathway from recreational involvement to competitive readiness.

02.3

Establishment of the EKSUMSA Run Club

Create a student-led running community that promotes fitness and shared recreational activity.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Organise periodic group runs and accessible running activities; welcome different fitness and experience levels with sensible routes and safety arrangements. Explore campus runs, friendly challenges and inter-school running engagements where feasible.

EXPECTED IMPACT

A low-barrier route into physical activity and a stronger culture of recreational fitness.

02.4

Inter-Class Sports Development and Engagement

Strengthen class-level sporting organisation and encourage friendly participation between classes.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Work with class sports representatives to communicate training opportunities and encourage involvement. Support informal inter-class activities, practice sessions and friendly engagements. Use class representatives to identify participation barriers and communicate equipment or facility needs. Major association competitions remain under Pillar 3.

EXPECTED IMPACT

Stronger class representation and a more active grassroots environment without duplicating major tournaments.

02.5

Inclusive Sports Participation and Development

Promote meaningful opportunities for students who may otherwise be underrepresented in sporting activities.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Actively encourage female participation across available disciplines and recreational activities. Support preclinical and clinical students through suitable arrangements. Create welcoming entry points for beginners and development opportunities for experienced students. Promote recreational participation alongside competitive pathways within available capacity and safe conditions.

EXPECTED IMPACT

A broader participation base that recognises different interests, schedules and levels of experience.

MEASURABLE DELIVERABLES

  • Current register of sports captains, coaches and training leads across active disciplines.
  • Published and updated weekly training schedules, adjusted when academic or facility needs require.
  • Basic attendance and activity records for organised training sessions.
  • Records of Run Club activities and inter-class engagement.
  • Evidence of participation opportunities for female, preclinical and clinical students.
  • Documented process distinguishing open training access from competitive team selection.
03

PILLAR 03

Competitive Sports & Sporting Events

Strengthening EKSUMSA's competitive sporting culture through well-organised competitions, established tournaments and opportunities for sporting excellence.

STRATEGIC OBJECTIVE

Deliver engaging and well-administered sporting events that allow students to compete, represent their classes and association, develop sporting excellence and build lasting traditions.

03.1

EKSUMSA Super Cup

Establish a proposed annual football fixture between the reigning EKSUMSA League champions and the HW Cup champions.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Coordinate with relevant organisers to confirm teams and date. Use the fixture to test improvements in officiating, match administration and presentation. Trial match statistics, player-performance monitoring and live video coverage where resources allow, then assess which innovations can be adopted in future league editions.

EXPECTED IMPACT

A high-profile testing platform for future league improvements, subject to team availability and coordination.

03.2

EKSUMSA League V

Deliver the fifth edition of the EKSUMSA Men's Football League.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Plan fixtures, match administration, team communication and rules in advance. Strengthen match-day administration and officiating, improve the reliability of team and player statistics, use the digital platform and media arrangements in Pillar 6, and document recommendations for future organisers.

EXPECTED IMPACT

A more organised and better-documented fifth edition of EKSUMSA's men's competition.

03.3

Dr Adebule Badminton Open

Offer interested students a structured competitive badminton event.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Set the format according to registration, facilities and resources. Publish rules, fixtures and match arrangements in advance, coordinate officiating and recognise strong performances.

EXPECTED IMPACT

A visible competitive platform for badminton.

03.4

Female Sports Fiesta

Create a dedicated sporting event that celebrates and expands female participation in EKSUMSA sports.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Develop a feasible programme of sporting activities in consultation with prospective participants and available organisers. Conclude the fiesta with a Female Football Tournament as its featured final competition.

EXPECTED IMPACT

A visible platform for female sporting participation, competition and recognition.

03.5

EKSUMSA Volleyball Competition

Organise an internal volleyball competition for interested EKSUMSA participants.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Select a format according to registration, available teams, facilities and equipment. Establish rules, fixtures, officiating and participant communication. Document results and identify ways to develop volleyball participation.

EXPECTED IMPACT

A stronger internal volleyball competition pathway. External volleyball friendlies are addressed under Pillar 4.

03.6

EKSUMSA e-Football League

Create a competitive platform for students interested in electronic football and esports.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Set registration requirements, competition rules and a suitable format. Confirm venue, equipment and match arrangements before opening the competition. Explore collaboration or support where equipment and event resources are limiting factors.

EXPECTED IMPACT

Recognition of esports as part of student sporting interests and an organised platform for competition.

03.7

EKSUMSA Ballon d'Or

Recognise the Best Footballer of the Sporting Year in EKSUMSA.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Define eligibility, nomination and assessment criteria before the award process. Consider performance, contribution and achievements across the sporting year using documented evidence. Apply a transparent assessment process and communicate its basis.

EXPECTED IMPACT

A credible football-specific individual honour celebrating sustained performance and contribution; it is not a general award for all sports.

03.8

Health Week Sports Collaboration

Support relevant sporting activities organised as part of Health Week, in agreement with the Health Week organisers.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Agree responsibilities with the Health Week organising team before committing resources or arrangements. Provide practical support within the Directorate's capacity and agreed role. The Prof. Odu Badminton Tournament remains part of the Health Week Sports programme, not a separate Sports Directorate tournament.

EXPECTED IMPACT

Better coordination and support for association-wide sporting activities while respecting the jurisdiction of Health Week organisers.

MEASURABLE DELIVERABLES

  • Event plans, rules, fixtures and communication records for competitions delivered.
  • Verified results and competition reports for the Super Cup, League V and other organised events.
  • Female Sports Fiesta programme culminating in the Female Football Tournament, subject to feasibility.
  • Published format and results for the Volleyball Competition and e-Football League where delivered.
  • Published eligibility and assessment criteria, nominations and outcome record for the EKSUMSA Ballon d'Or.
  • Record of agreed Sports Directorate support for Health Week sporting activities.
04

PILLAR 04

Inter-Faculty, Inter-Departmental & Institutional Sporting Engagement

Expanding EKSUMSA's sporting reach through inter-departmental competitions, university-wide engagements and relationships with other medical students' associations.

STRATEGIC OBJECTIVE

Strengthen EKSUMSA's position within the wider university and medical-student sporting environment through structured friendly competition, collaboration and institutional engagement.

04.1

Provost Cup - An Inter-Faculty and Inter-Departmental Sporting Festival

Develop the Provost Cup into a recurring multi-sport festival, initially bringing together EKSUMSA and other departments within the College of Medicine.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Begin with practical engagement involving departments such as Anatomy, Physiology and Medical Biochemistry alongside EKSUMSA. Explore expansion to other EKSU faculties and departments as participation, resources and institutional support allow. Include football, basketball, volleyball, badminton and other feasible sports. Establish fixtures, rules, scoring, departmental standings and recognition; engage departmental leadership and university authorities. Pursue a recurring tradition subject to institutional support.

EXPECTED IMPACT

A broader platform for inter-departmental interaction and competition, with the potential to become a lasting university sporting tradition.

04.2

Inter-MSA Sporting Friendlies

Build relationships with Medical Students' Associations at other medical schools through periodic sporting friendlies.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Explore reciprocal fixtures in football, volleyball and other suitable team sports. Coordinate dates, teams, rules, venues and logistics with interested MSAs. Use engagements to build relationships and competitive experience and support preparation for possible NiMSA Games participation. Consider reciprocal hosting where practical and mutually agreed.

EXPECTED IMPACT

Stronger inter-MSA relationships and additional competitive experience; fixture frequency depends on partner availability and logistics.

04.3

EKSU Institutional Sporting Friendlies (EXO)

Develop sporting engagement with official EKSU university teams, commonly referred to as EXO.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Explore friendly fixtures with official EKSU teams in football, volleyball, basketball and other suitable sports. Coordinate dates, venues, rules and participation with relevant team officials and university structures. Use engagements to strengthen institutional relationships and provide wider competitive exposure.

EXPECTED IMPACT

Greater connection between EKSUMSA and the university sporting structure. EXO means official EKSU teams and is distinct from EKSUMSA teams.

MEASURABLE DELIVERABLES

  • Concept and stakeholder-engagement plan for the Provost Cup.
  • Record of participating departments, approved sports and event outcomes where delivered.
  • Records of inter-MSA and EXO engagement proposals, confirmed fixtures and results.
  • Documented permissions, agreements and logistical requirements for external engagements.
  • Event review identifying opportunities for future expansion or institutionalisation.
05

PILLAR 05

Strategic Sporting Partnerships, Accessibility & Competitive Development

Leveraging strategic sporting relationships and access to technical expertise to strengthen development, improve training access and prepare EKSUMSA athletes for future competition.

STRATEGIC OBJECTIVE

Use existing university and state sporting structures to improve technical development and make training more accessible to students based at different academic locations, while strengthening preparation for external competitions.

05.1

University Sporting Team Partnerships and Decentralised Training

Explore collaboration with official EKSU basketball, volleyball and handball teams, using suitable locations to improve access for preclinical and clinical students.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

For preclinical students based at the university, explore training access through relevant EKSU teams and facilities. For clinical students at the Teaching Hospital, pursue suitable nearby opportunities, including the Teaching Hospital volleyball court. Explore clinical basketball development through engagement with the Ekiti State Basketball Team at the Pavilion, subject to agreement. Keep routine training decentralised around students' academic locations; organise occasional joint sessions, approximately once or twice monthly where practicable, to build cohesion.

EXPECTED IMPACT

More accessible training and better team cohesion without assuming transport support or guaranteed external-facility access.

05.2

Basketball Technical Development Partnership

Engage the Ekiti State Basketball Coach and state team at the Pavilion to explore specialist guidance.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Seek coaching guidance in fundamentals, skills, tactics and team organisation. Explore suitable training sessions, technical support and competitive-development opportunities where available and appropriate.

EXPECTED IMPACT

Improved access to technical expertise and more structured basketball development, subject to availability and agreement.

05.3

Athletics Development and Technical Training Partnership

Explore engagement with the Ekiti State Athletics Team to improve access to track-and-field expertise.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Seek stadium-based training and technical guidance in suitable events. Encourage participation across academic levels and experience groups, coordinating sessions around facility access, academic commitments and technical personnel.

EXPECTED IMPACT

A more structured route into athletics and better exposure to technical training, subject to partnership and facility arrangements.

05.4

Structured Competitive Development and NiMSA Games 2027 Readiness

Strengthen preparation across available disciplines for possible external representation, including the NiMSA Games 2027.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Work with captains and coaches to identify needs. Clarify eligibility, registration and selection requirements when official information is available. Prepare prospective representative athletes through additional training where feasible. Support participation planning only if EKSUMSA formally decides to participate and eligibility, funding and logistics are met. Continue grassroots and technical development regardless of that decision.

EXPECTED IMPACT

Better readiness for external competition. NiMSA Games 2027 participation remains conditional on the association's official decision and applicable requirements.

MEASURABLE DELIVERABLES

  • Record of partnership approaches, agreed access arrangements and relevant contact persons.
  • Decentralised training approach distinguishing regular local sessions from occasional joint sessions.
  • Records of technical-development sessions and partner involvement where agreed.
  • Summary of eligibility, preparation and registration requirements for possible NiMSA Games participation.
  • Representative-team preparation plan based on available coaches, facilities and resources.
06

PILLAR 06

Sports Innovation, Digital Technology & Media Development

Modernising EKSUMSA sports through digital innovation, systematic documentation, improved media coverage and technology in competition management.

STRATEGIC OBJECTIVE

Improve administration, visibility and historical documentation through a dedicated league platform, better football performance tracking, selective live coverage and a Sports Media Team documenting every sporting event organised or supported by the Directorate.

06.1

EKSUMSA League Digital Platform - eksumsaleague.com

Develop and maintain a digital platform primarily dedicated to the EKSUMSA Men's Football League.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Provide accessible fixtures, results, standings, team and player profiles, competition statistics and match reports. Publish photographs and selected highlights subject to capacity and permissions. Build a record of previous league editions and notable developments, with a supporting section for achievements and innovations in other EKSUMSA sports. Assign responsibility for updates, maintenance and continuity.

EXPECTED IMPACT

A central source of league information and a more reliable historical record. The platform is not intended to be a comprehensive association-wide sports-management system.

06.2

Football Statistics and League Performance Tracking

Strengthen statistics and performance documentation for the EKSUMSA Men's Football League.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Track goals, assists, appearances, team performance, match records and competition progression. Use consistent recording and verification methods, maintain historical records where information is available, and use documented information to support football recognition such as the EKSUMSA Ballon d'Or. Keep the initial programme focused on men's football; future administrations may consider expansion.

EXPECTED IMPACT

More reliable football records, improved performance analysis and a stronger evidence base for recognition and reporting.

06.3

Match of the Week - Live Coverage and Match Highlights

Introduce selective live coverage and stronger visual documentation for the EKSUMSA Men's Football League.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Select a featured Match of the Week for live coverage when personnel, equipment, connectivity and other requirements permit. Improve photography across league fixtures, produce selected highlights where capacity allows, and prepare match summaries and post-match reports. Build broadcasting capability progressively; live coverage of every fixture is not promised.

EXPECTED IMPACT

Greater league visibility, improved documentation and a better experience for supporters unable to attend every fixture.

06.4

Establishment of an EKSUMSA Sports Media Team

Establish a dedicated media team under the Sports Directorate to document every sporting event organised or supported by the Directorate.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Cover football, basketball, volleyball, badminton, athletics, handball and other supported sports. Organise practical roles for media coordination, photography, videography/live streaming, writing/reporting and graphics/digital content. Produce announcements, previews, match reports, interviews, highlights and athlete/team features. Maintain a digital archive and coordinate with the EKSUMSA PRO or Director of Public Relations. Apply appropriate consent and responsible handling of images and personal performance information.

EXPECTED IMPACT

Consistent documentation across sports and a stronger public record. Detailed statistics and selective live broadcasting remain primarily football-focused.

MEASURABLE DELIVERABLES

  • Functioning EKSUMSA League platform with maintained fixtures, results, standings and league information.
  • Consistent football statistics record and documented verification approach.
  • Selected Match of the Week coverage record, plus photography and reports for league fixtures as resources allow.
  • Sports Media Team roster with defined roles and coverage responsibilities.
  • Digital archive of events organised or supported by the Directorate.
  • Evidence of coordination with the wider EKSUMSA public-relations structure.
07

PILLAR 07

Sports Culture, Community & Engagement

Promoting a vibrant sporting culture through intellectual engagement, social activities, community participation and celebration of shared sporting interests.

STRATEGIC OBJECTIVE

Strengthen the place of sports within the EKSUMSA community by creating opportunities for students to connect, engage and celebrate sports beyond active participation in competitions.

07.1

EKSUMSA Sports Quiz and Debate

Organise a sports-themed intellectual engagement in collaboration with the EKSUMSA Quiz and Debate Club.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Plan a combined programme featuring sports quiz and debate sessions. Develop questions and topics covering football, basketball, athletics, badminton, sports history, major sporting events and wider sporting issues. Encourage participation across levels, provide clear rules and judging arrangements, recognise outstanding participants and coordinate responsibilities with the Club in advance.

EXPECTED IMPACT

A platform for students who engage with sports intellectually, greater awareness of different disciplines and stronger collaboration between association groups.

07.2

EKSUMSA Jersey Night

Conclude the Sports Quiz and Debate programme with a sporting-themed social event organised with the Social Directorate.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Hold Jersey Night on the same day as the Sports Quiz and Debate as its grand finale. Encourage students to wear jerseys representing favourite clubs, national teams or other sporting identities. Include suitable sporting interactions, entertainment and opportunities to connect. Agree planning, publicity and delivery responsibilities with the Social Directorate.

EXPECTED IMPACT

A recognisable social finale connecting intellectual sporting engagement with student social life.

07.3

Building a Vibrant EKSUMSA Sporting Community

Encourage a culture in which students support, appreciate and identify with EKSUMSA sports.

View implementation plan and expected impact

IMPLEMENTATION

Encourage attendance at sporting competitions and activities. Promote positive rivalries, respect for opponents and healthy competition. Celebrate athletes, teams and organisers; encourage community participation alongside playing, coaching and officiating. Use the Sports Media Team to document sporting stories while the Directorate drives participation and community engagement.

EXPECTED IMPACT

Greater attendance, stronger belonging and a sporting culture extending beyond those on the field or court.

MEASURABLE DELIVERABLES

  • Jointly developed programme for the Sports Quiz and Debate.
  • Jersey Night finale held on the same day, subject to agreed collaboration and resources.
  • Participation and outcome records for quiz and debate activities.
  • Evidence of student-support and community-engagement efforts around EKSUMSA sporting events.
  • Record of sporting achievements and community highlights communicated during the session.

FROM PLAN TO DELIVERY

IMPLEMENTATION,
ACCOUNTABILITY & CONTINUITY.

The seven pillars will be translated into practical activity through a deliberate, documented process.

01

Establish the baseline

Review existing equipment, active sports, facilities, class and sports representatives, partnerships, digital assets and the association event calendar. Identify what can be continued, improved or introduced.

02

Prioritise according to need and capacity

Sequence activities according to student demand, safety, academic commitments, personnel, equipment, facilities and funding. When resources are limited, prioritise essential equipment, regular participation and well-planned events before resource-intensive expansion.

03

Coordinate stakeholders early

Engage class representatives, captains, coaches, clubs, departmental representatives, university authorities, EXO teams, other MSAs, state sporting bodies and external partners before announcing activities that require their participation or approval.

04

Make responsibilities clear

Assign an identified lead for each activity and agree supporting roles for captains, coaches, class representatives, event organisers and the Sports Media Team. For joint programmes, document the responsibilities of the Directorate and collaborating body.

05

Document delivery and outcomes

Maintain practical records of equipment, training, participation, fixtures, results, partnerships, media coverage, event reviews and resources used. These records support accountability, improve future editions and reduce loss of institutional knowledge.

06

Review, adapt and hand over

Review initiatives against stated deliverables and adapt when participation, funding, facilities or academic conditions change. Prepare an organised handover containing registers, digital access information, event records, partner contacts, outstanding commitments and recommendations.

BOUNDARIES & DELIVERY CONDITIONS

  • The Directorate can organise, coordinate, advocate and document; it cannot independently guarantee university facility upgrades, institutional adoption, external sponsorship or access to third-party facilities.
  • Training access through university or state teams, including use of the Pavilion or other facilities, depends on partner agreement, availability, permissions and logistics.
  • NiMSA Games 2027 participation depends on EKSUMSA's official decision, applicable eligibility and registration requirements, funding and logistics.
  • Live streaming, video production and website development will be delivered according to available equipment, connectivity, personnel and technical capacity.
  • Event formats, dates and scale will be confirmed after assessing student registration, academic commitments, venues, officiating and resources.
  • The Prof. Odu Badminton Tournament is part of the Health Week Sports programme; the Sports Directorate may support it only in an agreed collaborative role.
  • The proposed initial football allocation of at least one ball per class is a planning target subject to inventory, class coverage and available resources.

CLOSING COMMITMENT

The goal of this manifesto is not simply to increase the number of sporting events. It is to build a system in which students can access equipment, train consistently, compete fairly, connect with wider sporting institutions, see their achievements documented and feel part of a shared EKSUMSA sporting community.

With clear responsibilities, practical priorities and accountable collaboration, the seven pillars provide a framework for developing a stronger and more sustainable sporting culture.
