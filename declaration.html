EKSUMSA 2026/2027

DECLARATION OF INTENT

To take EKSUMSA sports to the next stage it deserves.

Fellow medical students,

It is with enthusiasm, a deep sense of responsibility and a firm belief in what we can achieve together that I formally declare my intention to contest for the position of Director of Sports, Ekiti State University Medical Students' Association (EKSUMSA), for the 2026/2027 session.

Sports is more than competition. It is a platform for unity, talent discovery, discipline, recreation, healthy rivalry and lasting memories. It brings students together beyond academic boundaries and gives each of us an opportunity to belong to something bigger.

I believe EKSUMSA sports has enormous potential. We can build on what already exists, address the areas that need attention and create an even more engaging sporting culture for our students.

My intention is not simply to occupy an office. It is to contribute meaningfully to the growth, structure, visibility and overall development of sports in our association.

THE DIRECTION

AN EKSUMSA SPORTING COMMUNITY WHERE…

01

OPPORTUNITIES ARE EXPANDED

More students have the opportunity to participate, showcase their abilities and feel included.

02

COMPETITIONS ARE BETTER ORGANISED

Sporting activities are structured, competitive, engaging and worth anticipating.

03

TALENT IS RECOGNISED

Ability is identified, encouraged and given room to develop and flourish.

04

UNITY IS STRENGTHENED

Sports becomes an even stronger avenue for connection, friendship and shared identity.

05

OUR SPORTING REPUTATION GROWS

EKSUMSA is recognised as an association that takes sporting excellence seriously, within and beyond the university.

Leadership comes with expectations, responsibilities and challenges. I do not make this declaration under the illusion that the journey will be effortless. I make it with the conviction that commitment, collaboration, innovation and purposeful leadership can produce meaningful progress.

I am not coming with a promise of perfection. I am coming with the willingness to work, the courage to innovate, the readiness to listen and the determination to deliver.

I ask for your trust, your support and the opportunity to serve.

Together, we can build on what has been achieved, improve what needs attention and create something future generations of EKSUMSA students will be proud to inherit.

THE AMBITION IS BIGGER.
THE VISION IS CLEARER.
THE WORK MUST BE GREATER.

I am ready for the responsibility.
